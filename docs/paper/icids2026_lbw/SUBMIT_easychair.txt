=== Title ===
Measuring Emotional Trajectory Divergence in Interactive Narrative: Alignment and Contamination Metrics in Project M

=== Abstract ===
Interactive narratives promise every participant a different experience, yet that difference is almost always described as a difference in plot branches. We present Project M (PM), a working browser-based interactive narrative in which the participant's emotional trajectory, not a branching graph, determines which scenes become accessible each turn, together with two computable metrics for how that trajectory departs from the author's: a real-time alignment score that compares the shape of emotional change independently of its level, and a two-axis contamination vector that separates directional drift from single-point fixation. Trajectories that reach contact with the author's are converted automatically into interpretive fragments for later participants. A 193-play persona simulation shows that the alignment metric discriminates personality profiles (r = 0.55–0.63) while exposing a ceiling bias in LLM-simulated readers; a three-participant human pilot (21 scene-plays) exhibits the low tail the simulation lacked and shows three readers diverging onto distinct scene sequences without any authored branch.
